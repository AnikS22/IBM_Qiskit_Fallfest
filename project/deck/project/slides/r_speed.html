How to read it: across is the size of the head grid, up is the time for one answer at 5% accuracy, both on log scales. Red is classical: it climbs steeply because every voxel has to be updated at every step. Blue is quantum: all voxels update in the same layer of gates, so each step costs a flat 41.8 microseconds. The lines cross at about 283 cubed voxels, and the full head at 440 cubed is past that, about 3 times faster. Be clear that past 135 qubits this is a projection on error-free hardware, and today’s chip still loses most of the signal.
