The whole story in numbers. Accuracy: the circuit is right to about half a percent. Noise control: twirling cut error 78 percent, filtering about 75. Real hardware: one job on ibm_marrakesh, 24,000 shots, 0.081 against an exact 0.104 after filtering. Speed: one circuit run is about 110 times faster than the classical solve, and at full-head scale we project quantum about 3 times faster, on error-free hardware.
