Compiled 2026-10-17. Quotes were checked against the primary text where possible. PubMed abstracts came through NCBI E-utilities. The AHA 2026 Statistics Update PDF (632 pp) and the full text of the 2026 AHA/ASA guideline were read directly from ahajournals.org.

**Pitch point:** about 1 in 8 US strokes and about 1 in 3 strokes worldwide are bleeds. Clot-busting drugs are harmful in a bleed, so the scan has to come first.

## Slide mapping

**Slide 3: Problem: clot or bleed?**
- 87% of US strokes are clots and 13% are bleeds (AHA-2026). Globally, 29% are intracerebral hemorrhage (GBD-2021).
- "Brain imaging is essential to exclude hemorrhage" before reperfusion therapy (AIS-2026 §3.2).
- 1.9 million neurons are lost per minute (Saver 2006).
- IV thrombolysis must start within 4.5 h; thrombectomy within 6–24 h (AIS-2026).
- Only 25% of patients arrive within 3 h (Tong 2012).
- Hook quote: the guideline lists ultrasound among emerging portable detectors whose "clinical use remains uncertain" (AIS-2026 §2.3).

**Slide 4: Stakeholders**
- Patients:
  - 795,000 US strokes/yr (AHA-2026).
  - 11.9M strokes and 7.3M deaths/yr globally (GBD-2021).
- Paramedics:
  - Door-to-imaging is 26–31 min even after arrival (AIS-2026, GWTG).
  - Call-to-CT is roughly 50–60+ min (composite; label it an estimate).
  - Only about 2 dozen CT ambulances operate in the US.
- Rural hospitals:
  - 37.7% of Americans (113M) lack 60-min access to thrombectomy (JNIS 2022).
  - About 20% (49M) live more than 60 min from advanced stroke care, 65% of them rural (CDC PCD 2025).
  - Transfer door-in-door-out takes a median 174 min, and only 27% meet the 120-min target (AIS-2026).
- Health systems:
  - $56.2B/yr US (CDC).
  - $890B/yr global (WSO).
  - Lifetime cost is about $104k per patient in 1990 dollars (Taylor 1996).
  - An MSU costs about $1M to build plus about $0.8M/yr to run, at a borderline ICER (Ann Neurol 2025).
