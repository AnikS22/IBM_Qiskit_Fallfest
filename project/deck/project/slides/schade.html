Quantum Rationale & Understanding of Potential

The 1D quantum algorithm we build on: how a wave runs on qubits.

1

Discretize the wave

1D elastic wave on a grid, with varying density ρ and stiffness μ

2

Rewrite as Schrödinger

Velocity and strain stack into |ψ⟩; i∂ₜ|ψ⟩ = H|ψ⟩

3

Load the wave

2ⁿ grid points live in just n qubits

4

Evolve

Apply e^(−iHt): the wave moves inside the qubits

5

Measure

Repeat and count to read the wavefield back

64 grid points → 6 qubits. A whole head’s 341M sites → 29.

Schade, Bösch, Hapla & Fichtner, arXiv:2312.14747v2 (2024), Appendix figure, CC BY 4.0
