Quantum Rationale & Understanding of Potential

Conclusions & limitations: what we found, and what still limits us.

What we found

Ideal simulation had 0.58% mean-trace pressure error, while each individual ideal repeat still exceeded 1%.

On hardware, twirling cut pressure error 78% across 3 pairs; filtering cut energy error ~75% for one observable in one job.

Simulated quantum annealing had similar error to classical annealing but took ~10× longer on a CPU.

What still limits us

Adding 0.1% gate noise raised simulator pressure error to 8.85%.

Filtering retained only 16.3% of shots at 8 steps; extra ZNE correction did not establish better accuracy.

Small synthetic tests and limited hardware repeats do not establish clinical imaging or end-to-end speed and cost gains.

A wave-simulation prototype with useful error controls; larger reconstruction tests come next.
