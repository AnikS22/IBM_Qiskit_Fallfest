Signed pressure on IBM Kingston: gate twirling cut pressure error 78%.

HOW TO READ IT

a · Each point is one pressure value: hardware (up) vs the noiseless answer (across). On the dotted line = perfect. Grey = no twirling, blue = gate twirling. b · Average pressure error per run; lines join matched pairs.

WHAT IT SHOWS

Twirling turns structured gate errors into random ones that average out. Mean error fell from 0.664 to 0.148 Pa (−78%), improving all 3 matched pairs. 6 IBM Kingston jobs. Readout correction made all 6 worse; residual error remains.
