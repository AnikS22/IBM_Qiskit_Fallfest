Quantum Rationale & Understanding of Potential

The science: a brain image is a map of how fast sound travels.

1

Voxels

c = (c₁, …, c_N)

~10⁸ cubes of 0.5 mm, each one sound speed.

2

Travel time

t ≈ Σ ℓᵢ / cᵢ

Crossing paths triangulate each cube.

3

Full waveform

19 mm → 0.9 mm

Using the whole wave sharpens the image 20×.

That sharpness is exactly what makes the computation so expensive.
