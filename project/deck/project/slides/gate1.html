Technical Implementation (Qiskit)

Our gate-based quantum algorithm (1/2): given a tissue map and a pulse, predict where the wave energy goes.

The larger imaging loop adjusts the tissue map to match real sensor recordings. This circuit answers one question inside it.

01

Guess tissue map

Assign sound speeds to the cells. Start a known ultrasound pulse.

02

Store wave in qubits

Quantum gates move the simulated wave between neighbouring sites.

03

Energy at brain site

Repeat the circuit. Count how often the selected brain site is measured.

Hardware demo: receiver energy. Pressure reconstruction uses a separate compact circuit.

Team code: team-quantum-ultrasound · after Costa 2019, Schade et al. 2024

Three steps. Guess the map and start the pulse; the gates carry the wave site to site; we rerun and count how often the brain site lights up. That count is the wave energy there.
