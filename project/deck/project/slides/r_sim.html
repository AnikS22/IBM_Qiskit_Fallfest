Results & Validation

Simulator check: our quantum circuit reproduces the wave, until noise is added.

HOW TO READ IT

a · Bar height = how far the circuit’s pressure is from the exact answer (lower is better; dotted line = 1% goal). Dots = the 3 repeat runs. b · Where each method lands when it tries to recover a tissue speed: start at 1.00, truth at 1.10.

WHAT IT SHOWS

Ideal circuit: 0.58% error on the averaged trace, but each single run was above 1%. Add 0.1% gate noise and error jumps to 8.85%, and the noisy run walks the speed the wrong way. Small synthetic pilot.

Team repo IBM_Qiskit_Hackathon · runs/gate_ideal, runs/gate_noisy (Qiskit Aer, 3 repeats each)

Panel a is accuracy. Ideal circuit lands within about half a percent on average; realistic noise makes it 15 times worse. Panel b is the real test: can each method move the speed guess from 1.00 toward the true 1.10? Classical and ideal-gate move the right way; noisy moves backwards. Noise is the enemy.
